About the System

This Academic Decision Support System uses machine learning to predict student academic performance based on internal marks, attendance, and assignment completion. The system helps identify at-risk students early and provides personalized recommendations for improvement. Teachers can upload entire class data for comprehensive analytics, while students can get individual performance insights.

Prediction Goals: Categorizing students into performance groups, predicting semester results, identifying subject-wise weak areas, estimating future performance trends, and analyzing the impact of attendance and study habits on outcomes.
